Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit nrnworld.one. In short: you do not need an account, we do not ask for personal details to browse the site, and the only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to send us in a message.

1. Who is responsible

nRnWorld (Robin Ayzit) runs this website. For any privacy question or request, contact bynrnworld@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your device

The site keeps a few preferences in your browser's localStorage so the interface works the way you left it. These stay on your device, are never sent to us, and disappear if you clear your browser data:

  • nrnworld_saved_projects_v1 – projects you saved
  • nrnworld_starred_projects_v1 – projects you starred locally
  • nrnworld_language_v1 – your chosen language

We do not set our own tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not build visitor profiles.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. When ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick/Google advertising cookie) and similar technologies to measure ad performance and, where you have consented, to personalise the ads you see. Ads are also served on pages that display the AdSense script.

  • Google's use of advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Opt out of personalised ads: adssettings.google.com and aboutads.info
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only receive personalised advertising after giving consent through the consent message shown by Google; without consent, advertising is limited to non-personalised ads. You can withdraw or change that choice at any time through the links above.

4. Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics, which reports aggregate page views without cookies and without identifying individual visitors. Project download, play and open counters are also stored as plain numbers only — they contain no personal data and are never linked to a person.

5. When you contact us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, optional link and message you provide. That information is used solely to read and answer your message, is kept only as long as needed for that purpose, and is never sold or used for marketing. Contact form submissions are delivered through Web3Forms (web3forms.com/privacy), which processes the message on our behalf.

6. External services and content

Loading certain pages fetches content from third parties, which see your IP address:

  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Vercel Analytics — aggregate visitor statistics
  • GitHub — project statistics, release links and downloads
  • Spline (spline.design) — the animated 3D scene on the home page
  • media.gitgit.me — the decorative background video

Download links point to GitHub releases on github.com/nRn-World, and other links lead to external sites. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

7. Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests for the technical operation and security of the site, on consent for personalised advertising, and on your request when you send us a message. You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

8. Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Security and international transfers

The site is served over HTTPS and hosted on GitHub Pages and Vercel. Some providers listed above may process data outside the EU/EEA, relying on the standard contractual clauses of those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features change. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest revision, and material changes will be reflected here.
